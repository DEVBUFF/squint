# Brand identity

## What an identity system contains
- **Logo system**: symbol, wordmark, lockups, clear space, minimum sizes, app icon / favicon variants.
- **Colour**: primary, secondary, neutrals, functional (success / warning / error / info), with roles.
- **Typography**: display, text, optional mono; scale; rules for weights and case.
- **Shape & material**: radii, borders, depth/shadows/glass/grain, pattern or graphic devices.
- **Imagery**: photography direction, illustration style, 3D, iconography.
- **Motion**: easing personality, signature transitions, logo animation.
- **Voice**: tone attributes, vocabulary, naming conventions.
- **Sound** (optional): notification / brand sounds.

## Audit questions
1. **Distinctive** — cover the logo: is it still recognisably this brand? Or could it be any startup?
2. **Coherent** — site, app, store listing, icon, emails, social: one brand or several?
3. **Appropriate** — does it signal the right price point, seriousness and audience?
4. **Scalable** — works at 16 px favicon and on a billboard, light and dark, monochrome.
5. **Ownable** — avoids the look-alike patterns flagged as `peaking` in the branding trend file.
6. **Accessible** — brand colours pass contrast in their actual roles; type is legible.

## Refresh vs. rebrand
- **Refresh** (evolve type, colour, details; keep equity) when the brand is recognised but the
  execution is dated or inconsistent. Default choice.
- **Rebrand** when the positioning changed, the brand carries negative equity, the name/legal forces
  it, or it's so generic there is nothing to keep. Present as a Bold option with risks.

## Practical rules
- One hero colour + neutrals + at most one supporting accent is easier to own than a rainbow.
- Custom or less-common typefaces are the fastest way to distinctiveness; check licences (OFL/Google
  Fonts are safe; commercial fonts need the user's decision).
- App icon: one idea, strong silhouette, test at 60 px on light, dark and tinted wallpapers.
- Generated imagery: fine for exploration; for shipped brand assets prefer a consistent, ownable style
  and say when a human illustrator/photographer would add value.
- Don't change the name or logo without an explicit request — propose, never apply.
