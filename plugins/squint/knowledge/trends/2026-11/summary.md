# Design trends digest · November 2026

Platform files: [web](web.md) · [ios](ios.md) · [android](android.md) · [branding](branding.md)
(58 trends, 280 source entries, many carried over from October).

## The month in five lines
1. **Foldables became real.** iPhone Duo went on sale 2026-10-23 and Apple opened optimized-app submissions. Android Dev Summit (2026-10-28) pushed adaptive layouts and said Play will scrutinise foldable behaviour in 2027.
2. **CSS-native motion crossed the line.** Firefox 159 shipped scroll-driven animations by default on 2026-10-27, so all three engines now have them.
3. **Agentic UI is first-party on Android.** A2UI for Jetpack Compose (alpha) was announced, with Compose 1.13 adding Grid, FlexBox and Progressive Blur.
4. **Branding keeps evolving, not erasing.** Nu (ex-Nubank, Koto, 2026-10-29) kept its logo and wordmark and moved distinctiveness into type, illustration and motion.
5. **Everything else held steady.** 48 of 58 trends are `→`; none are new and none moved to Fading.

## Safe bets vs. risky bets
- **Safe:** adaptive layouts and system toolbars on iOS and Android, CSS-native scroll and view transitions (with a fallback), custom type families, evolving an identity instead of replacing it.
- **Risky or generic:** bento feature grids (peaking), Chrome-only features (`focusgroup`, `text-fit`, `border-area`) without a fallback, and AI-orb marks.
- **Unconfirmed:** Compose 1.13 and Material3 1.5 are still beta, so check stability before depending on them.

## Positioning & branding signals
- Nu's refresh shows that a category-wide colour (fintech purple) is not enough. Ownable type, illustration and motion carry the distinction.
- Masterbrand consolidation continues (Nubank shortened to "Nu" for the US launch).
- Brisbane 2032 emblem: critics split (B&T, 2026-10-08), while organisers cite 38M views. This is a watchlist item, not a trend.
- Gaps: Pantone's 2027 colour is not announced, and the Apple Music icon status in stable iOS 27.2 is unconfirmed.
